# Repair and sign off per-layer PDP telemetry in NVDLA

A deployment team cannot trust performance samples collected from consecutive pooling layers. Tensor results remain correct, but a quiet layer can report stalls left over from its predecessor. The profiler also lacks denominators and useful-work counts, making it difficult to distinguish request pressure from downstream drain time. Diagnose the seed RTL and deliver reliable, software-readable telemetry for the fixed nv_small configuration.

Repair the existing PDP and PDP_RDMA stall statistics, then implement the banked request-phase cycle and accepted-atom registers specified in SPEC.md. Integrate the supplied NV_PDP_perf_epoch interface with both datapaths and their CSB register paths. Determine the correct observation points, launch ownership and publication boundaries by tracing the generated RTL, configuration and actual tool output. Read commands describe variable amounts of work; write commands and data packets have different meanings. Register producer selection may change while hardware executes a queued group.

Each completed request phase publishes a coherent, saturating snapshot to its executing group. Preserve the preceding snapshot while that group runs again. Disabled measurement publishes zero on completion. Reset clears pending measurement and both snapshots. Include accepted work on the completion edge, and keep request-phase duration distinct from full-operation completion and external AXI stalls.

Deliver your RTL, a concise causal diagnosis, an explanation of the sampling and snapshot design, and reproducible regressions. You may modify rtl/ and add your own tests and documentation. Preserve the existing datapath behavior, original ports, observation boundaries, software register map and the new component interface. The fixed workload generator, AXI service, driver, monitor, toolchain and grader are outside the edit scope. Alternative synthesizable counter organizations and equivalent CSB integration are welcome.

Acceptance regenerates and compiles the complete Verilator model from your RTL. It compares actual CSB reads with independently reconstructed request events, exercises queued groups, disabled layers, live reads, read-only protection and saturation, and checks real tensor dumps, guards and upstream CRC controls. A report, dimension-based estimate or passing component alone cannot establish integration correctness. Use the normal build, execution and component-probe commands supplied with the project.
